## Everything assigned to you

One list, every organization. This is the page to work from day to day.

## Moving work along

Change a task's status as you go. What you set here is what the organization sees on their dashboard and in their reports - updating status is not admin overhead, it is the only signal they have.

## Logging time

Record hours against a task while you still remember them.

Time reports on the organization's side are built entirely from these entries. Work you did but never logged does not appear anywhere as work.

## Subtasks

Some tasks are broken into subtasks. Ticking them off shows progress inside the task; the task itself still needs its status moved when it is genuinely done.

## What you cannot do here

Task assignment, projects and teams are managed by the organization. If something is assigned to you that should not be, the fix is a conversation, not a setting.
