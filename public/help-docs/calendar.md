## What the calendar shows

Dated work and scheduled meetings on one timeline. The calendar is a **view**, not a separate store - it does not hold anything of its own.

That means two things appear here automatically:

- **Tasks with a due date**
- **Scheduled meetings**

## Why something is missing

Nearly always: the task has **no due date**. A task without one is real work and appears everywhere else in TaskFlow, but the calendar has nowhere to draw it.

## Workload

The workload view shows who is over-committed in the period you are looking at. It counts dated tasks only - for the same reason as above.

Worth checking before assigning more work, and worth taking seriously when it disagrees with your instinct about who has capacity.
