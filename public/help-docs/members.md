## How someone becomes a member

You invite them by email, choosing the role they should hold. They receive a link and join with that role's permissions.

Until they accept, they are a **pending invitation** - not a member. They cannot be assigned tasks or added to teams yet.

## Roles, not people

A member's access comes entirely from their role. There are no per-person permissions in TaskFlow, by design: it means you can always answer "what can this person do?" by looking at one role instead of auditing an individual.

To give one member more access you have two honest options:

1. Move them to a role that already has it.
2. Add the permission to their current role - and accept that everyone else in that role gets it too.

If neither is right, the role is probably missing. Create a new one.

## The owner

Whoever created the organization is its owner, and always has full access. The owner cannot be locked out by a role change - which is what stops a workspace becoming unmanageable after a permissions mistake.

## Removing someone

Removing a member takes away their access. Work already assigned to them stays in the system; reassign it so it does not become invisible.
