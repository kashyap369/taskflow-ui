## Invitations sent to you

This page shows invitations you have **received**, not ones you sent. Invitations you have sent are on the Members page of your organization.

An Organization account can be invited into another organization too - the invitation is addressed to you as a person, not to a portal.

## Accepting

Accepting makes you a member of that organization, holding the role they chose for you. The workspace then appears in your switcher, and their tasks assigned to you start showing up in your task list.

## Declining

Declining removes the invitation. The organization can send another one later if it was a mistake.

## An invitation that never arrived

Check the address it was sent to. An invitation is tied to a specific email, and it only appears here for the account that owns that address.
