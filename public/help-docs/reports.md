## What reports are made of

Reports enter nothing of their own. Every figure is derived from data you already have:

- **Tasks** and their statuses
- **Time logged** against those tasks
- The **members, teams and projects** they belong to

## Choosing what to measure

Pick a period and a view - by member, by team, or by project. Reports answer a specific question well and answer "everything at once" poorly, so it is worth narrowing before reading.

## The trend line

Completion across the period. A flat line usually does not mean nothing happened; it means **tasks are not being moved to done**. That is worth checking before drawing conclusions about the team.

## Time reports read as empty

If time reports look empty, the work was almost certainly done without being logged. Time reports are built entirely from work-log entries on tasks - there is no other source they could use.
