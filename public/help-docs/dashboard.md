## What this page is

A summary of the organization named in the workspace switcher. If you belong to more than one organization, everything on the dashboard changes when you switch.

## The four numbers

- **Projects** - how many are currently running.
- **In progress** - tasks that have been started but are not finished.
- **Completed** - tasks moved to done.
- **Overdue** - tasks whose due date has passed while still open. This is the one to act on.

Overdue is not a separate status you set. A task becomes overdue on its own when its due date passes, and stops being overdue the moment it is completed or re-dated.

## If the numbers look wrong

Almost always one of these:

- You are looking at a **different workspace** than you think. Check the switcher.
- Tasks are being finished but **not moved to done**, so completion stays flat.
- The work has no due dates, so nothing can ever register as overdue.
