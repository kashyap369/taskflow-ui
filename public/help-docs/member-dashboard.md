## Your personal workspace

This side of TaskFlow is yours. It gathers work assigned to you across **every** organization you belong to, rather than making you check each one.

## What you see

- What is assigned to you
- What you have in progress
- What has gone overdue

All of it pooled across organizations. If a number looks higher than you expect, it is probably counting a workspace you had forgotten you were in.

## Getting around

- **My tasks** is the day-to-day page.
- **Projects** shows the projects your work belongs to.
- **Invitations** is where new organizations reach you.
