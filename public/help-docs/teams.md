## What a team is

A named group of members - Engineering, Design, a client pod. Teams exist to organise people and to give reports a useful grouping.

## What teams are not

**Teams do not grant permissions.** This is the most common misunderstanding in TaskFlow. Adding someone to a team changes nothing about what they are allowed to do; that comes from their role, always.

## Membership

A person can be in several teams, or none. Being in no team does not stop anyone working - it only means they will not appear under a team heading in reports.

To add someone to a team they must already be a member of the organization. If they are not, invite them first.

## Teams and reporting

Team reports roll up the tasks and logged time of everyone in the team. If a team's report looks empty, check that its members are the ones actually assigned the work.
