## Meetings in your workspace

Video meetings scheduled in TaskFlow appear on your calendar, invite your members by name, and can admit people from outside your organization as guests.

## Scheduling

Give the meeting a title, a time and participants. Members are invited directly. Anyone outside the organization joins through a **guest link** you create afterwards.

## Joining

The join button appears close to the start time. If it is missing, either the meeting has not opened yet, or you are not on the participant list.

## Guests

Guest links are for people outside your organization - a client, a candidate, a contractor.

Two things worth knowing:

- A link can be **revoked**, and stops working immediately when you do.
- Guests see the meeting and nothing else. They get no access to your projects, tasks or members.

## Badges

Display badges are labels shown next to names in the call - "Host", "Client", "Observer" - so people can tell who they are talking to. Useful the moment a call has more than a couple of outsiders in it.

## Recording

Recordings appear on the meeting's page after it ends.

Everyone in the call is told when recording is on. Consent is asked for rather than assumed, and that is not a setting you can turn off - it is how the feature works.

## The meeting room

Once you join, the room is full-screen. The help button is deliberately hidden there so nothing floats over the video.
