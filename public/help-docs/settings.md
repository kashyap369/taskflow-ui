## What lives here

Details about the organization itself: its name and description, as your members and guests see them. Changing the name does not affect any existing work.

## What does not live here

Permissions. Those are on the **Roles** page - settings is about the organization, roles are about what people may do inside it.

## Deleting the organization

This removes the workspace and everything filed under it: projects, tasks, teams, meetings and reports.

It is not a per-item delete and it is not a way to tidy up. Read the confirmation carefully. If you want to stop using a workspace without destroying its history, remove its members instead.
