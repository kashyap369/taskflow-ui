How to use TaskFlow

Every page in TaskFlow also explains itself — look for the Help button in the bottom-right corner and it will walk you through whichever page you are on. This is the longer version.

How it fits together

Six things, in the order you set them up. Each one depends on the one before it.

  1. 1

    Organization

    Your workspace. Everything below belongs to it, and whoever created it is the owner.

  2. 2

    Roles

    What a kind of member may do. Permissions are granted here and nowhere else.

  3. 3

    Members

    People who accepted an invitation. Each holds one role, which is where their access comes from.

  4. 4

    Teams

    Groups of members, for organising and reporting. Teams do not grant permissions — roles do.

  5. 5

    Projects

    Containers for related work, with their own dates and progress.

  6. 6

    Tasks & subtasks

    The actual work. A task belongs to a project, is assigned to one member, and can be split into subtasks. Time is logged against tasks.

Calendar, Meetings and Reports read from all of this. They store almost nothing of their own — which is why a task with no due date never appears on the calendar, and why unlogged work shows as no work in the time reports.

How your workspace works

  1. 1

    Invitations

    Organizations invite you. Accepting one makes you a member of their workspace.

  2. 2

    My tasks

    Work assigned to you, gathered from every organization you belong to.

  3. 3

    Time logs

    Hours you record against a task. These are what the organization’s time reports are built from.

}
